Product metrics

How to Measure What Matters

Every action has two sides: doing and measuring. How to choose the few product vital signs worth tracking.

Introduction

Every action has two sides: doing and measuring. Just like checking your temperature tells you about your health, measuring your product’s vital signs reveals its performance and well-being. This article explores the importance of product measurement and how to choose the right metrics to track.

The Importance of Measurement

Imagine a doctor without a thermometer or a driver without an odometer. Measuring is crucial in various aspects of life, and product health is no exception.

Human Health

Body temperature, heart rate, and blood pressure are vital signs that indicate health issues. Similarly, product metrics like active users and churn rate reveal potential problems.

Car Health

The odometer shows the car’s overall condition, while the check engine light indicates trouble. Product dashboards provide similar insights, highlighting areas for improvement.

Choosing the Right Metrics

Each product is unique, so there’s no one-size-fits-all approach to measurement. Instead of drowning in data, focus on key metrics that align with your product’s goals.

Gather Stakeholders

Bring together product managers, designers, and other stakeholders to brainstorm relevant metrics that reflect your product’s mission and target audience.

Prioritize the “Odometer”

Identify a few critical metrics, similar to a car’s odometer, that provide a quick overview of your product’s health. These metrics should be easily accessible and readily understood.

Deeper Dives

When the “odometer” raises concerns, delve deeper into specific data points to diagnose the issue. This is akin to a doctor using diagnostic tools beyond basic vital signs.

Conclusion

By measuring the right product metrics, you gain valuable insights into its health and performance. This enables you to identify issues early, make informed decisions, and ultimately, build a successful product.
